# **App Name**: FoodBuzz

## Core Features:

- Restaurant Listing: Comprehensive listing of restaurants and cloud kitchens with menus, prices, and reviews.
- Food Quality Rating: Users can rate the food quality of their favorite restaurants.
- Feedback System: Users can provide feedback on their experiences. Implement sentiment analysis to categorize feedback (positive, negative, neutral) and suggest improvements powered by AI.
- Donation/Tips: Users can donate or tip their favorite restaurants.
- Rating and Comment System: Users can share their experiences through ratings and comments.
- Restaurant Suggestion: Suggest restaurants nearby based on user preferences and ratings.  Employ a collaborative filtering approach, suggesting restaurants favored by users with similar taste profiles. Also provide an AI powered system for restaurant owners to receive suggestions for menu options based on their ratings.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Saturated orange (#FF8C00) to evoke appetite and energy.
- Background color: Desaturated light orange (#FAEBD7) for a clean, inviting feel.
- Accent color: Yellow-orange (#FFA500) for highlights and calls to action, complementing the primary orange.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', a modern sans-serif, for an elegant, fashionable feel (headlines).
- Body font: 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif, when longer text is anticipated (body).
- Use food-related icons with a modern, minimalist design.
- Subtle animations for loading and transitioning between screens to enhance user experience.
